# Sentences by phrase (all videos)

Every video is a set of sentence **groups**. Inside a group (usually 4 sentences, sometimes 3) all sentences practise the same phrase — the phrase is the group title. The pattern inside a group is normally: general statement → my routine → past experience → but these days.

No timestamps: play the video and follow along.

- On a daily basis, the café near my home is full of people in the morning.

- But nowadays, I like sleek cafes.

### ritual

- But these days, coffee is my daily ritual.

### neighborhood

- In my neighborhood, there is a small café.
- I enjoy spending time in neighborhood cafés.
- When I was young, there was a café with jazz music in my neighborhood.
- But these days, I love small cafes in my neighborhood.

### introverted

- I'm introverted, so I like quiet bus rides.
- I'm introverted, so I don't usually talk on the bus.
- One time I sat next to an extroverted person on the bus.
- But these days, I'm quite introverted.

### when it comes to commuting

- When it comes to commuting, I take the subway every day.
- When it came to commuting, I walked to school.
- But these days, when it comes to commuting, I choose the subway.

### public transportation

- I use public transportation every day to go to work.
- I once took public transportation, and it was crowded.
- But these days, I use public transportation all the time.

### bus fare

- I pay 2 dollars for the bus fare every day.
- I once was annoyed because I didn't have enough for the bus fare.
- But these days, the subway fare is too expensive.

### subway lines

- My city has numerous subway lines.
- I usually check the subway lines on my phone before I go to work.
- I once got lost because I took the wrong subway line.
- But these days, so many new subway lines have been built.

### meticulous about recycling

- People in my country are meticulous about recycling.
- I try to be meticulous when I recycle at home.
- I remember once I was meticulous about recycling but the truck didn't come that day.
- These days, you need to be meticulous when it comes to recycling.

### down to the last detail

- Rules are explained down to the last detail.
- At home, I follow the guide down to the last detail.
- My teacher explained recycling down to the last detail.
- These days, recycling is regulated down to the last detail.

### food waste

- Food waste is used as compost in my country.
- I separate food waste to make compost.
- At school, I once made compost from food waste.
- Before, people didn't make compost at home.

### go the extra mile

- In my country, some people go the extra mile by washing all the bottles before recycling.
- I always go the extra mile by checking the labels before throwing things away.
- One time, I went the extra mile by separating everything, but the truck didn't come.
- In the past, people didn't go the extra mile with recycling.

### vinyl

- In my country, people separate vinyl bags from other kinds of trash.
- At home, I always recycle vinyl by putting it in a clear bag.
- When I was young, I didn't know vinyl had to be recycled separately.
- In the past, people often threw vinyl away instead of recycling it.

### inventions

- Whoever invented AirPods, I really appreciate them.
- I usually enjoy looking up videos about great inventions on YouTube.
- Engineers invented a lot of new machines to make life easier.
- But these days, we're surrounded by so many amazing inventions.

### rehearse

- I've realized that doing a camera rehearsal is really important.
- As a YouTuber, I always rehearse before shooting.
- I once skipped a dress rehearsal before a live company event, and I totally regretted it.
- But now, I try to rehearse every single time before an important event.

### impressive

- I think electronics these days are pretty impressive.
- I often buy the newest tech products that really impress me.
- When I was in middle school, I thought MP3 players were extremely impressive.
- But now, some gadgets, like MP3 players, aren't impressive anymore.

### usable

- My laptop is pretty cheap, but it's still very usable.
- I check my headphones almost every morning to make sure they're usable throughout the day.
- I thought it would be usable, but it turned out to be completely broken.
- So now, I always charge my wireless mouse to make sure it's usable when I need it.

### value

- I value my iPad more than any other gadget I own.
- I always bring my MacBook with me because I really value it.
- My MP3 player was the most valuable thing I had when I was younger.
- These days, I really value how quickly technology is developing.

### look forward to

- I look forward to hanging out with my friends during my free time.
- I tend to look forward to the weekend when I'm at work.
- I was once so sick and looked forward to a speedy recovery.
- But these days, I tend to look forward to major holidays like Christmas.

### staying in

- I truly believe staying in is the best way to spend your free time.
- Staying in and doing nothing is how I usually spend my free time.
- I once had some free time and decided to just stay in.
- In the past, I preferred staying in to hanging out.

### hanging out with my friends during my free time

- I love hanging out with my friends during my free time.
- These days, I don't have enough free time to hang out with my friends.
- I have this vivid memory of hanging out with my friends during my free time.
- But now, I prefer hanging out with my family when I have free time.

### recharge

- Free time is the only time that I can fully recharge.
- I like to recharge at home whenever I have free time.
- Last Saturday, I decided to recharge at a cafe.
- Back when I was younger, I would read books to recharge during my free time.

### going for a walk

- Going for a walk is one of my favorite ways to spend free time.
- I always go for a walk whenever I have free time.
- I went for a walk last night with my wife and it was a great experience.
- But now, I've realized that going for a walk is a nice way to spend free time.
